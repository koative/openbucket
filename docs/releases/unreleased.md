OpenBucket is now **Bucketdeck**. The app, website ([bucketdeck.com](https://bucketdeck.com)), repository and Homebrew cask carry the new name; your connections, saved keys, favorites and settings stay where they are and open as before.

## Changed

- The app is called Bucketdeck and installs as `Bucketdeck.app`. After installing it, you can delete `OpenBucket.app`; both use the same settings, so don't run them side by side.
- New work is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Releases up to and including v0.2.3 remain under the MIT License.
- The About window lists the open-source packages built into the app, with their licenses and notices.
